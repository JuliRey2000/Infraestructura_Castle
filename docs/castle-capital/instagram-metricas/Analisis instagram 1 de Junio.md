\#\# 6\. ANALYSIS & INSIGHTS

\#\#\# ✅ What's Working (High Reach)

\*\*Reels drive virtually all discovery.\*\* Despite Historias generating slightly more raw visualizations (53% vs 46.6%), Reels account for 81.8% of all interactions — they are the primary engagement engine. Reels also attract 10.9% of interactions from non-followers, suggesting strong algorithmic distribution.

\*\*Mindset and personal development themes reach beyond your existing audience.\*\* Reel \#1 ("No arriesgar") achieved 58% non-follower views; Reel \#2 ("VUELVETE VALIOSO") hit 67.1% non-follower views. These emotionally resonant, universally relatable topics — self-worth, risk, courage — pull in cold audiences more effectively than finance-specific content likely does.

\*\*The audience is highly engaged in reach-to-interaction ratio.\*\* 160 accounts interacting out of 2,027 reached \= a 7.9% interaction rate on reached accounts, which is strong for a sub-600 follower account.

\#\#\# ❌ What's Not Working (Low Conversion)

\*\*Profile visits are not converting to followers.\*\* 286 profile visits with only 588 total followers (and the top Reel generating 0 new followers) indicates a serious conversion gap. Visitors are arriving but not following — likely because the bio or grid doesn't clearly communicate ongoing value or a compelling reason to follow now.

\*\*Publicaciones (static posts) are essentially dead.\*\* Only 0.4% of visualizations and 10.9% of interactions come from static posts. This format is not receiving algorithmic distribution and is consuming creation effort with minimal return.

\*\*Historias get views but almost no engagement.\*\* 53% of total visualizations but only 7.3% of interactions — followers watch Stories but don't respond to them. Story content likely lacks interactive elements (polls, questions, CTAs) that drive engagement metrics.

\*\*Non-follower interactions remain low (10.9%).\*\* Despite nearly half of all views coming from non-followers, they account for only 10.9% of interactions. The content is attracting them but not compelling enough action to convert.

\#\#\# 🎯 Prioritized Action Items for the Next 24 Hours

\*\*Action 1 — Post a Reel at 15:00 today (Monday).\*\* This is the single highest-activity window across the entire week (199 followers active). The content should follow the mindset/personal development format that performed best (not purely finance/trading, which likely appeals to a narrower audience). Suggested hook format: a bold, counterintuitive statement about money, risk, or success in the first 2 seconds.

\*\*Action 2 — Add a "Follow" CTA inside the Reel itself.\*\* Neither of the top Reels converted profile visits to followers (Reel \#1 generated 0 new followers despite 969 accounts reached). Record a verbal CTA mid-reel: "Si esto te resonó, sígueme — publico esto cada semana." This directly addresses the conversion gap.

\*\*Action 3 — Optimize the bio for conversion.\*\* The current bio ("Sígueme y escríbeme · Guía GRATIS 👇") has a CTA but it's competing with multiple other messages. Restructure to: (1) Who you help, (2) Specific transformation offered, (3) Single clear CTA. Example: "📈 Enseño a personas normales a hacer trading rentable | Guía GRATIS en el link 👇"

\*\*Action 4 — Add an interactive Story today after posting the Reel.\*\* Post a Story within 2 hours of the Reel using a poll or question sticker directly related to the Reel's topic (e.g., "¿Cuál es tu mayor miedo al invertir?"). This will spike Story engagement metrics and signal to the algorithm that your audience is actively engaged, boosting the Reel's distribution window.

\*\*Action 5 — Stop investing in static Publicaciones until Reels are scaled.\*\* With 0.4% of visualizations, static posts are not worth the effort at this stage. Redirect that time into more Reels or improving Story interaction rates.
