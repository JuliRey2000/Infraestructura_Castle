\#\# 5\. KEY PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

\#\#\# What's Working ✅

\*\*Reels dominate reach (51.1% of views, 75.6% of engagement).\*\* The format is working and generating organic discovery — both top Reels had more than 57% non-follower views, proving strong distribution via the Explore and Reels feed. The mindset/motivation Reel on May 5 outperformed the technical chart Reel on all metrics: more views (+20.7%), higher reach (+28.7%), and nearly 3× the shares (9 vs 2). Motivational content with personal presence clearly resonates more with non-followers.

\*\*Non-follower reach is strong at 51%.\*\* This means the algorithm is actively distributing the content beyond the existing audience, which is the key lever for growth at this stage.

\*\*Consistent daily posting cadence.\*\* The top-5 performing content spans across the month (April 28, May 1, 4, 5, 13, 18), suggesting reasonable publishing frequency.

\#\#\# What's Not Working ❌

\*\*Zero to near-zero follower conversion from Reels.\*\* The \#1 Reel by views (1,789 views, 964 reached accounts) generated 0 new followers. The \#2 Reel (1,482 views, 749 reached) generated only 1 follower. This is a critical gap: the account is reaching new audiences but failing to convert them. The conversion rate from non-follower views to follows is effectively 0–0.1%.

\*\*Historias generate almost no engagement (6.7% of interactions) despite accounting for 48.5% of views.\*\* Stories are consuming nearly half the impression count but delivering almost no engagement action. This represents a significant content efficiency problem.

\*\*Publicaciones (feed posts) are nearly invisible at 0.3% of views.\*\* Static feed posts are receiving essentially no distribution.

\*\*Profile visit-to-follower conversion is failing.\*\* 327 profile visits in 30 days with net near-zero follower gains from content means the profile itself is not compelling enough to convert curious viewers into followers.

\*\*Very low "Guardados" (saves).\*\* The top Reel had only 1 save out of 1,789 views. Saves are a key signal Instagram uses to boost content distribution — low saves tell the algorithm the content isn't valuable enough to bookmark.

\#\# 6\. PRIORITIZED ACTION ITEMS (Next 24 Hours)

\*\*Priority 1 — Post a new Reel TODAY at 15:00 (Tue–Fri) or 14:45.\*\*  
The 15h window is the single highest-activity period across every day of the week (196–198 active followers). Post Reels during this window to maximize initial velocity, which directly influences algorithmic distribution.

\*\*Priority 2 — Add a clear CTA inside the Reel to drive saves.\*\*  
At the end of every Reel, add: \*"Guarda este video para cuando lo necesites"\* (Save this video for when you need it). A save rate improvement from \~0% to even 2% would significantly boost the algorithm's read of content quality and expand distribution.

\*\*Priority 3 — Add a direct follow CTA in every Reel's first 3 seconds.\*\*  
Neither top Reel converted non-follower viewers to followers. The hook must explicitly invite action: \*"Si esto te sirve, sígueme — subo esto cada semana"\*. The follow prompt must appear verbally AND in on-screen text early in the video.

\*\*Priority 4 — Fix the bio's conversion funnel.\*\*  
With 327 profile visits and near-zero conversion, the profile link and bio must work harder. The bio currently ends with "Guía GRATIS 👇" — make sure the link actually delivers a lead magnet. A frictionless opt-in (WhatsApp, linktree with free guide, etc.) converts profile visits into warm leads and/or followers.

\*\*Priority 5 — Produce more mindset/motivational content, reduce chart-only content.\*\*  
Reel \#1 (motivacional / talking head) outperformed Reel \#2 (chart overlay) by 20% in views and 52% in engagements, and generated 4.5× more shares. Shares are the primary amplification lever on Reels — produce 2 motivational Reels for every 1 technical analysis Reel.

\*\*Priority 6 — Reduce or repurpose Historias.\*\*  
Stories represent 48.5% of views but only 6.7% of interactions. If the main KPI is reach and follower growth, Stories should be repurposed to redirect followers to the latest Reel ("¿Ya viste mi último Reel? 👇"), not to generate original views. This realigns the Story function with growth goals.
